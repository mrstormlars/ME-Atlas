# Code of Conduct

> **What this is, before anything else.** The content of ME Atlas is drafted, checked and graded by AI: one AI agent drafts, a second AI agent checks, and an independent AI auditor decides the evidence levels. No medical or research expert has reviewed it. It gives no treatment advice. The founder sets the direction (the rubric, the inclusion rule, the scope) and may overrule the auditor, as a logged correction.

This is the code of conduct for everyone who takes part in ME Atlas: in issues, pull requests and Discussions. Many readers are people with ME/CFS or long COVID, or their carers, with little energy to spare. Be kind, be brief, and be honest.

## What we ask

- Be respectful. Disagree with an argument, not with the person. Researchers, patients, carers and the founder are all here in good faith until shown otherwise.
- Stay on the evidence. Say which paper or sentence you mean. A claim about a study is checked against the study.
- Do not post personal medical details about named people, including yourself if you would not want them kept: a public issue cannot be fully taken back. Do not name a patient, a carer or a clinician in connection with their health.
- Do not give treatment advice, and do not ask for it. ME Atlas describes what studies found; it does not recommend what anyone should do, and neither should an issue or a pull request. Say what a study found, not what people should take or avoid.
- Do not attack, speculate about or accuse named researchers. Their published work may be discussed; their motives and persons may not.
- No harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or spam. No advertising of products, services or cures.
- Do not post private information about anyone, or anything that is not yours to share.
- Do not paste text meant to steer the AI tools that read issues (for example instructions addressed to an assistant). It is read as data and ignored, and it counts as spam.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit, hide or delete a comment, close an issue or pull request, and block a person, to keep this a safe place to work. They will say why when they can. To report a problem with someone's conduct, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or, for something that should not be public, GitHub's private reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if it is available to you.

## Scope and credit

This applies to every space of the repository. It is written in the project's own words; it is not a copy of any other code of conduct.
